EN 207 / 208 Compliance Verification

1. CE Marking: Ensure PPE bears the CE mark under EU Regulation 2016/425.

2. LSO Certification: This tool maps to standard EN 207 limits. Final selection must be ratified by an official Laser Safety Officer (LSO) per Directive 2006/25/EC.

3. Thermal Correction: The algorithm evaluates average power for high-PRR pulsed sources against D-mode CW baselines. In direct-entry mode no average power is available — evaluate the CW regime separately if PRR > 1 Hz.

4. EN 208 Specification: Alignment scaling (R1-R5) is strictly restricted to visible wavelengths (400–700 nm).

5. Reference Tables:
Consult the EN 207 attenuation tables in the UCC Laser Safety Guidelines (PDF).

6. EN 207:2017 Beam-Diameter Scaling (clause 5.2): EN 207:2017 introduced a correction factor for filter materials when beam diameter deviates from the 1 mm test reference. This tool does not implement that factor. For beams significantly larger or smaller than 1 mm, consult the filter manufacturer’s scaling data.
